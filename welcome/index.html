Welcome

Hello, and welcome to Tiny Bytes

Most people use AI to answer questions. But it can also make things: checklists you can tick, pages that count down, little tools for your family or business. In every issue we'll show you one you probably didn't know it could do. Copy the prompt, pop it into the AI app on your phone, and see what you get. It takes a minute, and there's no coding.

It works with whichever AI you already use: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot or something else. If a build needs a specific app or a paid plan, we'll say so up front.

Tiny Bytes comes from Pick & Mix Studios. We build game-inspired eLearning for a living, and we believe learning should feel a bit like play. This newsletter is that idea, pointed at AI.
